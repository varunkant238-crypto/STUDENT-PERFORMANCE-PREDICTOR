Scientific Feature Engineering

The 5 Validated Input Features

Carefully selected via Pearson Correlation and Mutual Information analysis from the Kaggle student dataset to prevent target leakage and ensure practical ease-of-use.

Study Hours per Week

Tracks dedicated self-study hours outside lecture times (10 to 40 hrs/week). Proven moderate positive correlation with final assessment scores.

Attendance Rate (%)

Class attendance percentage. Emerged as the highest Mutual Information predictor (0.685), directly determining continuous concept grasp.

Past Exam Scores

Prior test achievement marks (50 to 100). Serves as the primary academic baseline, boasting a +0.490 Pearson correlation with the target.

Parental Education Level

High School, Bachelors, Masters, or PhD. Measures home academic support structure and socio-educational environment.

Extracurricular Activities

Participation in sports, clubs, and arts (Yes/No). Gauges holistic development, time management discipline, and mental stress relief.

Comprehensive Prediction

Three-Tier Prediction Architecture

Rather than an ambiguous numerical value alone, our system delivers actionable, multi-faceted feedback.

1. Predicted Exam Score

A continuous numerical score out of 100 marks generated by our fine-tuned Random Forest & Gradient Boosting regression models.

2. Pass / Fail Status

Rigorous threshold classification where scores $\ge 60$ marks pass, directly calibrated with the ground truth dataset distribution.

3. Graded Category

Qualitative tiering: Excellent (A), Good (B), Average (C), or Needs Improvement (F) with customized recommendations.
